AssumptionThe 02A/02B/02C split in Prompt 02 does not name Permissions introduction, AI personalization, or the new Ready screen; all are assigned to 02C as the post-auth onboarding stretch.

v1.1 — review amendments applied (REVIEW-v1.md, 2026-08-06)

  • Reorder: AI introduction → Complete profile → AI personalization, so Gurman knows name, city, and language before asking preferences.
  • Interests reframed: planning-intent question (“Help Gurman understand you”) with option rows — no Pinterest-style category grid.
  • Permissions: every row now leads with value (“Location — to recommend nearby experiences”).
  • AI introduction: promise-led opening (“Tell me what you’re planning. I’ll handle the rest.”) — no AI self-explanation.
  • New screen: Ready — a two-second, deliberately understated completion moment marking the end of onboarding, before Home (no confetti, no gamification — confidence, not excitement).
  • First home arrival: must answer “What can I do?” immediately — every widget carries guidance even with zero data.

v1.2 — product decisions applied (REVIEW-v1.md, 2026-08-06)

  • Guest mode approved: “Continue as guest” wires to First home arrival in limited mode — browse, search, view, and read are open; planning, booking, and social actions prompt account creation, never force it.
  • Camera removed from onboarding: permissions list is Location · Notifications · Gallery · Microphone (optional); camera is requested in context on the first camera-dependent action.
  • Interests vs. personalization split: AUTH-015 captures today’s planning intent (single-select, seeds the session); AUTH-016 builds the long-term preference profile (persistent Gurman memory) — no overlapping options.
