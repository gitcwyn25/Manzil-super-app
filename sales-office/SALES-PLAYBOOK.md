# Sales Playbook — Manzil Business

## ICP (ideal customer profile)

Tashkent SME with walk-in customers and a reputation to manage:

1. **Restaurants / cafes / choyxonas** (highest review volume, promo-hungry)
2. **Beauty & barbers** (booking-shaped, loyal repeat customers)
3. **Clinics & dental** (trust-driven — reviews decide)
4. **Services** (repair, cleaning, tutoring)

Owner profile: 25–50, runs the business from a phone, lives in Telegram, skeptical of "another platform," moved by *what customers say publicly about them*.

## District-density strategy

Win one district at a time. A consumer opening Manzil in Chilonzor must see a **full** map; an owner must see their **neighbors already on it**. Order: Chilonzor → Yunusobod → Mirobod → Yakkasaroy → outward. Do not spread thin across the whole city.

## The pitch (uz, doorstep version)

> "Assalomu alaykum. Manzil'da sizning sahifangiz allaqachon bor — mijozlar sizni ko'rib, baho qoldirishyapti. Sahifani o'zingiz boshqarishingiz uchun tasdiqlab beraman, bepul. Sharhlarga javob berasiz, ma'lumotni yangilaysiz. Ikki daqiqa."

Why it works: it's a **claim**, not a signup. The page exists; the owner is taking control of something already public.

## Pipeline stages

| Stage | Definition | Exit criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Listed | Seeded listing, unclaimed | — |
| Contacted | Doorstep visit / Telegram message | Owner responded |
| Claimed | Owner verified + owns the page | Logged into dashboard once |
| Activated | Replied to a review OR updated info | Weekly active |
| Paying | Pro/Max subscription | Payment received |
| Expanded | Multi-branch / annual | — |

## Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "I already have Instagram" | Instagram is your megaphone; Manzil is where people *compare and choose*. Both. The free tier costs you nothing. |
| "2GIS lists me already" | 2GIS shows where you are. Manzil shows *why you* — reviews, replies, promos. And you can't answer a customer on 2GIS the way you can here. |
| "Reviews scare me" | Reviews about you already exist — in Telegram groups you can't see. Here you see them and answer them. Silence is the only losing move. |
| "What does it cost?" | Free to claim and reply, forever. Pro (399k/oy) is only worth it once you see your weekly stats — I'll show you in a month. |
| "Can you delete a bad review?" | No — and that's why customers trust the ones that praise you. We remove only fake/abusive ones, by policy, for everyone. |

## Upgrade triggers (Free → Pro)

- Weekly views > 200 → "your customers are here" message with the stats screenshot
- 3+ reviews in a month → promo tools pitch (answering isn't enough; give them a reason to return)
- Competitor in same district goes Pro → search-priority pitch (Max)

## Rules

- Never promise review removal. Never discount below admin-set pricing without CFO sign-off.
- Every claim gets a Telegram follow-up within 24h (activation is the real sale).
