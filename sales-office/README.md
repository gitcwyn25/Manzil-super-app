## The motion (summary)

1. **Density beats spread.** Win one district at a time (start: Chilonzor) so consumers see a full map and owners see their neighbors.
2. **Claim, don't cold-create.** Listings are pre-seeded; the pitch is "your page already exists — claim it," which converts far better than "sign up for another platform."
3. **Free tier is the wedge**, review-reply is the hook (owners cannot ignore what customers say publicly), Pro is the upsell once weekly stats show real traffic.
4. **Telegram-first follow-up.** Owners answer Telegram, not email.
