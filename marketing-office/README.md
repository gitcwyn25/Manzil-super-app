## Brand rules (short version)

- **Teal + gold, committed.** No hedging into generic SaaS purple/neutral.
- **The arch is structure, not a sticker** — the Samarkand portal frames heroes and sections.
- **Say the real thing.** Official, plain, local voice; no hype, no emoji, no "do this do that" filler.
- **Telegram-first market.** Owners live in Telegram; campaigns meet them there.

## Channels (priority order)

1. Telegram (owner communities, channel + future CRM bot)
2. Instagram (consumer discovery, food/beauty verticals)
3. On-the-ground district activation (see sales-office playbook — marketing supplies materials)
4. App-store presence at mobile launch
