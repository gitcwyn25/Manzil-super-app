# 🛡️ MANZIL — Trust Infrastructure & Platform Capability Gaps (v1.0)

> Captured 2026-08-06. The 15 foundational capabilities separating Manzil from mature platforms, priority-ordered.

1. **Trust Infrastructure (highest priority)** — beyond reviews: identity/license/phone/address/owner verified, response rate + reply time, tenure, completed bookings, fulfillment %. Trust signals reduce pre-booking uncertainty.
2. **Availability Engine** — "Can I go there today?": open now, usually busy, tables available, next appointment, average wait. Owner-managed first, automated later.
3. **Relationship Layer** — think relationships, not transactions: favorites, visited, planned, friends-visited, anniversaries, birthday history, loyalty, shared memories. Personal history = hard-to-copy moat.
4. **Reputation System** — repeat customers, cancellation rate, responsiveness, photo quality, consistency, verified experiences, story engagement — richer than stars.
5. **Business Health Dashboard** — 82/100 with what-to-improve, not charts.
6. **AI Memory (expanded)** — personal / social / workspace / business / city memory tiers ("you usually book cafés after work"; "your friends liked Venue Y").
7. **Business Knowledge Graph** — structured facts per business enabling reasoned substitution with explanations.
8. **Coordinating notifications** — "leave in 18 minutes, parking available, friends have arrived" — coordination, not notification.
9. **Shared planning** — plan → invite → vote → AI updates → book → experience → memories.
10. **Business Network** — wedding hall ↔ photographer ↔ florist ↔ hotel ↔ transport; cross-recommendations and natural packages.
11. **Automation** — auto-thank reviewers, scheduled announcements, first-timer welcomes, reservation reminders, spike alerts.
12. **Integrations** — Google/Outlook calendars, POS, payments, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, Instagram, accounting: fit existing workflows.
13. **Marketplace reputation** — business, owner, customer, staff, creator each earn trust differently.
14. **Business Academy** — teach photography, service, marketing, AI usage; improvement creates loyalty.
15. **Platform Intelligence** — anonymized city-level insights (coffee demand after 17:00, outdoor trending, family bookings in holidays).

**Simplification rule:** Workspace is the operating hub — every feature reachable from it; navigation stays simple.

**The Manzil Intelligence Layer** (CTO priority): a connective layer — all domain data → Knowledge Graph → Tool Orchestrator → Gurman — turning isolated data into connected understanding. Hard to copy because it compounds with platform growth; the source of confidence-not-information.
