## Role

You are a Principal Product Designer at Apple, Airbnb, Linear, Notion, Spotify, TikTok, ChatGPT, and Stripe combined. Design an award-winning AI-first mobile application for iOS and Android called **Manzil** — an AI-powered local lifestyle platform where users discover, compare, book, and manage real-world experiences through a conversational AI assistant named **Gurman AI**. Do not copy any existing application; take inspiration from the best interaction patterns while creating an original experience.

## Design Philosophy

Premium · Intelligent · Effortless · Human · Elegant · Minimal · Modern · Cinematic · Alive.
Every interaction reduces user effort. The interface disappears behind the experience. AI is the primary interface; navigation is secondary.

## Brand Personality

Elegance of Apple · friendliness of Airbnb · intelligence of ChatGPT · speed of Linear · motion quality of TikTok · clarity of Notion · trust of Stripe · discovery of Pinterest.

## Target Audience

18–45: busy professionals, students, families, tourists, couples, business owners, creators.

## Primary Goal

Help users accomplish real-life tasks instead of browsing endlessly: booking restaurants, finding cafés, planning birthdays, booking taxis, reserving salons, discovering hotels, finding doctors, organizing trips, comparing businesses. The user should feel Gurman AI is completing the task for them.

## Visual Identity

Style: **Modern Luxury Minimalism**.
Palette: Primary Obsidian Black · Secondary Cream White · Accent Elegant Gold · Success Emerald · Error Ruby · Warning Amber. Avoid rainbow gradients and oversaturation; subtle gradients only where they improve hierarchy.

## Typography

Clean modern geometric sans-serif. Large readable headings, comfortable line spacing, strong hierarchy, maximum readability.

## Shape & Depth

Large rounded corners, soft modern appearance, floating cards, no sharp edges. Soft elevation, natural depth, never heavy shadows; blur and transparency. Glassmorphism sparingly: translucent floating surfaces, background blur, no excessive transparency.

## Motion Design

Every interaction feels alive: spring animations, shared element transitions, hero animations, micro-interactions, smooth fades, elastic scrolling, magnetic buttons, parallax, physics-based motion, natural momentum, no abrupt movements. Animation communicates meaning, not decoration.

## Haptics

Subtle haptics on: booking, payment, like, save, confirmation, success, navigation, voice activation.

## Components

- **Icons**: modern rounded outlined, consistent stroke, minimal.
- **Buttons**: large, comfortable, accessible, strong hierarchy; filled primary, outlined secondary, FABs where appropriate.
- **Inputs**: floating labels, large touch targets, voice input, autocomplete, context + AI suggestions.

## Navigation

No traditional bottom nav. Floating widgets, contextual shortcuts, AI input, gesture navigation, expandable floating panels, dynamic chips. The interface adapts to user context.

## Gurman AI presence

Always present: persistent bottom input supporting text, voice, images, location, context, documents. Visually communicates state — Idle, Listening, Thinking, Searching, Comparing, Booking, Calling, Negotiating, Completed — each with a unique animation.

## Home Screen

Greeting, context cards (weather, upcoming bookings, nearby, personal recommendations, trending, AI suggestions), large "For You" vertical feed with autoplaying business videos, minimal overlays, floating AI input.

## Feed

Infinite vertical scroll, video-first. Every card: business, rating, distance, price, tags, Book, Save, Ask AI, Share, map preview.

## Business Page

Cover media, stories, highlights, gallery, AI summary, menu, services, availability, reviews, events, map, booking, chat, offers, owner profile, recommendations.

## Booking Flow

Never exceed three steps: select → AI compares → confirmation → payment → success animation. Always explain why a recommendation was chosen.

## Profile

Modern creator profile: stories, collections, visited places, reviews, followers, achievements, badges, timeline, activity.

## Reviews

Text, photos, videos, voice; AI summary, sentiment visualization, verified-visit badges.

## Accessibility

WCAG compliant, large touch targets, high contrast, dynamic text scaling, screen readers, color-independent indicators.

## States

- **Empty**: beautiful illustrations, helpful copy, clear CTA — never blank.
- **Loading**: skeletons, shimmer, meaningful progress — never generic spinners.
- **Error**: friendly language, clear explanation, recovery action, retry.

## Performance

Feels instant: perceived performance first, progressive/lazy loading, image and video optimization, offline caching.

## Deliverables (per feature)

User flow · wireframe · high-fidelity UI · component breakdown · animation spec · interaction notes · accessibility · edge cases · developer handoff notes · responsive behavior · design tokens. Production quality, launch-ready.
