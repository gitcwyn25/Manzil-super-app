# 💰 MANZIL — Pricing, Acquisition & Communication Strategy (v1.0)

> Captured 2026-08-06. Don't sell listings — sell **growth and operations**. The business must always think: "Manzil helps me earn more than it costs."

## Pricing (three-sided)

1. **Free tier (Acquire)** — profile, photos, hours, reviews, stories, basic analytics, receive bookings, reply to reviews, limited announcements. Free forever; removes friction.
2. **Pro subscription (Grow)** — AI CRM, unlimited announcements, campaigns, segmentation, advanced analytics, Workspace Timeline, staff management, AI marketing + responses, priority ranking, reports. ~$20–50/mo market-adjusted.
3. **Success fees (Value)** — commission only where money is made: online bookings, event packages, tickets, gift cards, marketplace transactions. Commissions beat pay-to-exist.

Ads are separate (amplify, never replace organic discoverability). Future: **Gurman Business Assistant Pro** (demand prediction, pricing optimization, promotion suggestions, review analysis, campaign drafting) as premium AI.

## Consumer acquisition

Depth beats breadth: own one neighborhood → district → city → next city (Tashkent: Mirzo-Ulugbek → Yunusabad → Chilonzor). Loops: **Story loop** (visit → story → friends see → discover → visit → story), **Workspace loop** (birthday planned → workspace shared → 5 friends install → collaborate), **QR at every business** (scan → review → stories → follow → book again — businesses recruit users).

## Business acquisition

Phase 1 (0–500): manual, personal — call 100 not 10,000; categories with repeat visits + strong content (restaurants, coffee, barbers, beauty, events); **AI import onboarding** (owner confirms, doesn't type); local ambassadors (university students per district: photograph, onboard, explain, first stories, verify). Phase 2 (500–2k): referrals with credits. Phase 3 (2k–10k): outbound sales + associations. Phase 4 (10k+): network effects.

Value proposition: never "create a listing" — **"We'll help people discover, plan, and book experiences with you."**

## Communication (four levels)

1. Platform → Business (verification, reports, insights, features, billing — email/in-app/push)
2. Business → Customers (stories, announcements, events, offers, replies, messages, bookings)
3. AI → Business (advisor tone: "Tuesday bookings dropped", "reviews mention slow service", "launch a family promotion?")
4. Customer → Business (bookings, reviews, mentions, questions) — all in a **unified inbox** (messages, bookings, reviews, story mentions, campaign replies, notifications, AI suggestions — never five pages).

## Loyalty & health

Relationship-based loyalty first (visited → returned → shared → invited → reviewed), points later. **Business Health Score** (profile completion, response time, review quality, booking acceptance, media quality, verification, retention) drives improvement recommendations.

## Business Success Framework (feature gate for the business side)

**Acquire · Convert · Operate · Retain · Grow** — every business-facing feature must clearly serve at least one, or it doesn't belong.
