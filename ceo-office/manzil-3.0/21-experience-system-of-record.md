# 🧬 MANZIL — The Experience System of Record (v1.0)

> Captured 2026-08-06. Manzil is not a booking platform, CRM, or assistant — it is the **System of Record for Local Experiences** (as Maps is for places, Airbnb for stays, Shopify for commerce). Lifecycle expands: **Discover → Decide → Plan → Experience → Remember → Return.**

## The center of the database is the Experience

Not Business → bookings → reviews, but **Experience → businesses, people, timeline, AI, tasks, payments, memories**. A birthday is who/budget/distance/indoor/kids/parking/cake/photos/music/invitations → book → remind → memories → return next year — a graph, not a checklist.

## Experience Graph

Every experience type (birthday, wedding, date night, travel, family day…) connects businesses, services, tasks, timeline, friends, budget, payments, memories, reviews. Replacement = graph traversal under constraints (Italian · private room · Saturday · 20 guests · parking · ≤$200 · available), answered from understanding, with the diff explained ("keeps every requirement and adds outdoor seating").

## Facts, not filters

Business knowledge = structured facts (cuisine, price tier, parking, halal, vegetarian, kids menu, private room, live music, noise, wheelchair, pets, average stay, wait time, reservation policy, payments, Wi-Fi, outdoor, outlets, smoking, alcohol, delivery, hours, peak hours). **Services are first-class objects** (Haircut → provided by 120 businesses), making substitution trivial.

## Memory Graph

Not chat history — accreting structured memory: visited Restaurant A → with Sarah → birthday 2027 → Restaurant B birthday 2028. Workspace Timeline = Notion × Linear × Calendar; searchable years later (choose venue → invite → cake ✓ → paid → reminder → experience → photos → review → archive).

## AI answer path (LLM last, never first)

Question → intent detection → knowledge graph → workspace → business graph → availability → CRM → memory → Tool Orchestrator → **LLM** → answer.

## Trust Engine (dedicated service)

Identity, license, verification, response time, fulfillment rate, cancellations, complaints, refunds, review quality, repeat customers, tenure, verified photos/location/owner/staff → Trust Score (e.g. 96/100) → explainable recommendations.

## Marketplace Brain

The invisible layer answering: which businesses substitute for each other · which services are booked together · which neighborhoods are underserved · which campaigns drive repeat visits · who is likely planning a birthday next month · which businesses should collaborate · which listings are stale. Powers Gurman, search, recommendations, analytics, and business insights.

## Philosophy shift (adopted)

> **Manzil is the memory, planning, and operating system for local life.** Every experience leaves context; the next one gets easier; businesses learn from real interactions; Gurman understands relationships, not isolated facts.
