# Trust Center

Manzil has a simple promise: information shown to people should be checkable.

## Data and catalogue

Business profiles, services, and offers come from existing catalogue data. Report outdated or incorrect information through [contact](/en/contact).

## Reviews

Reviews are moderated. A review is not labelled as a verified visit without evidence of that visit. Owner replies are shown separately.

## Gurman AI

Gurman only shows options based on available businesses and structured evidence. The LLM does not rank, select, or score. When data is insufficient, the system says so clearly.

## Advertising and sponsorship

Paid placement is not presented as an organic recommendation. Advertising and sponsorship receive an explicit label.

## Responsibility

A Manzil recommendation is not a final decision. Confirm opening hours, price, and availability with the business before visiting.
