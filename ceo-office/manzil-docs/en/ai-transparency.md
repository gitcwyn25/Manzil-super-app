# Gurman AI: Transparency and Safety

Gurman AI is a recommendation feature that helps users find places and services in the Manzil directory.

## How It Works

The system analyzes the user’s query, selected city, directory information, and topics in available reviews to present suitable options. Results may vary depending on ratings, distance, atmosphere, price, and other criteria included in the query.

## Important Limitations

- AI information may be outdated, incomplete, or incorrect.
- A recommendation does not guarantee a place’s current availability, price, business hours, or quality.
- Confirm important information with the business before visiting.
- Gurman AI does not provide professional medical, legal, financial, or safety advice.
- An AI result must not be used as the sole basis for a legally significant decision affecting a person’s rights or legitimate interests.

## Advertising and Rankings

Paid placement, sponsorship, and advertising are labeled separately from organic recommendations. A business’s paid participation should not be grounds for secretly altering rankings or reviews.

## Feedback and Error Reporting

Report an incorrect recommendation, harmful response, or inaccurate directory information through the Contact page. Include the query, place name, problem, and a screenshot where possible; do not submit unnecessary personal information.

## Privacy

How information in AI queries is processed is described in the Privacy Policy. Do not submit special-category or excessive personal information.
