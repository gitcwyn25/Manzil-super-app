# Reviews and Community Guidelines

Manzil accepts reviews to facilitate the exchange of useful, honest, and respectful information about local places.

## What a Review Should Be Like

- based on personal experience;
- specific and relevant;
- an objective description of the service, atmosphere, price, cleanliness, or other verifiable aspects;
- include details that are useful to others.

## Prohibited Content

- fake, purchased, repetitive, or manipulative reviews;
- insults, threats, hatred, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- personal, confidential, or security-related information;
- promotion of illegal services, fraud, or dangerous activities;
- photographs, videos, or text that infringe another person’s copyright;
- coordinated activity intended to artificially increase or decrease a business’s rating.

## Moderation

Manzil may review a submission before or after publication using automated tools and human review. We may reject, edit, hide, or remove content. If content is removed, we will provide the reason and a way to appeal where possible.

## Reporting

Use the “Report” function next to the review or contact us through [Kontaktlar](/en/contact). We review reports concerning falsity, legal violations, privacy, copyright, threats, inaccurate business information, and conflicts of interest.

## Business Responses

Businesses may respond to reviews respectfully and based on facts. Threatening customers, disclosing their personal information, or pressuring them to remove a review is prohibited.

## Advertising and Conflicts of Interest

A reviewer who has a financial relationship with a business must disclose it. Paid placement and sponsorship are separately labeled in accordance with [Xizmatlardan foydalanish shartlari](/en/legal/terms).
