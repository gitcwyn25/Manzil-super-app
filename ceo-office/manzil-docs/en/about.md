# About Manzil

**Manzil is a platform for discovering real places and services in Uzbekistan.** We connect people with cafés, restaurants, service establishments, and local businesses in Tashkent and other cities.

Gurman AI helps users find suitable options by considering their natural-language requests, the atmosphere of a place, key points from reviews, price, and how well the options match their needs.

## What We Do

- Bring local places and services together in one directory.
- Make searching easier based on genuine reviews and information provided by businesses.
- Support natural-language requests in Uzbek, Russian, and English.
- Enable users to contact businesses, get directions, and compare options.
- Help local businesses present their services in a trustworthy and understandable way.

## Our Mission

To make it easier for people in Uzbekistan to find a good place and for local businesses to reach the right customers.

## Our Vision

To become a trusted, local, and intelligent digital guide for every city—creating an ecosystem where people make decisions, businesses grow, and city life is discovered more effectively.

## Our Operating Principles

1. **Local focus:** we respect the unique character of every city and neighborhood.
2. **Reliability:** we pay attention to the freshness of information and the quality of reviews.
3. **Transparency:** we present advertising, sponsorship, and AI recommendations in a way that is clear to users.
4. **User control:** we respect users’ data and choices.
5. **Fairness to businesses:** paid placement is not concealed as an organic recommendation.
