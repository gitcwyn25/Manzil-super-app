## Priority Recommendations

1. Make the “Trust and Legal” group clearly visible in the footer.
2. Display the effective date, last updated date, and the operator’s complete legal information on every legal page.
3. Make Uzbek the primary version and maintain the same substantive content across the Russian and English versions.
4. Do not present paid listings and organic recommendations in the same way; use an “Advertisement” or “Sponsored” label.
5. Add a visible warning such as “AI recommendation — verify before relying on it” and a “report an error” button to AI results.
6. Create a Privacy Center with methods for viewing, correcting, and deleting data, withdrawing consent, and opting out of marketing.
7. On business profiles, display the legal name, contact details, address, business hours, the QR code or number of any license/permit, prices, and the date last updated.
8. Before launch, clearly determine with a lawyer whether the directory’s actual model is an “information directory,” an “order aggregator,” or an “electronic trading platform.” If orders or payments are processed within Manzil, the requirements expand significantly.

## Uzbekistan Research Summary (2026-09-02)

- **Personal data:** Law of the Republic of Uzbekistan No. LRU-547 “On Personal Data,” dated July 2, 2019. LexUZ: https://lex.uz/en/docs/4396419 and https://lex.uz/docs/4831939. The draft privacy notice addresses purpose, consent, data-subject rights, security, confidentiality, retention, and the request channel.
- **2026 amendment:** LRU-1125, March 26, 2026, effective March 27, 2026. LexUZ: https://lex.uz/en/docs/8105926. Under Article 27-1, mandatory localization applies to biometric data, genetic data, and data concerning individuals who use the services of telecommunications operators operating in Uzbekistan. Before storing other information abroad, the basis of adequacy, standard clauses/corporate rules approved by the competent authority, or approved international standards must be verified.
- **Electronic commerce:** LRU-792, September 29, 2022. LexUZ: https://lex.uz/en/docs/6213428. Articles 3, 9, and 12–17 are important for the definition of a platform/operator, seller and operator information, electronic contracts, offers, document retention, and security.
- **Consumer rights:** 221-I, April 26, 1996, current text: https://lex.uz/docs/4525010. Articles 5–8, 10, 21, and 28-1 concern reliable information, prices/terms, unfair terms, and information requirements when trading through information systems.
- **Advertising:** LRU-776, June 7, 2022, effective September 9, 2022. LexUZ: https://lex.uz/en/docs/6052633. Articles 5, 16, 18, 31, 47, and 49 should be reviewed regarding misleading advertising, advertising identification, internet advertising, and liability.
- **Electronic commerce operator:** Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 885, December 26, 2024. LexUZ: https://lex.uz/en/docs/7285113. Operational issues such as the notification procedure effective from July 1, 2025, the requirement to be a legal entity resident in Uzbekistan, and separate bank accounts should be reviewed.
- **AI:** O‘RQ-1115, January 21, 2026. LexUZ: https://lex.uz/en/docs/8011930. Incorporate rules concerning the use of AI and human oversight of legally significant decisions into the technical product design. AI ethical rules, registration number 3787, LexUZ: https://lex.uz/ru/docs/8083770.

## Pre-Launch Legal Checklist

- [ ] Legal entity name, TIN, address, and representative have been confirmed.
- [ ] The “directory / order aggregator / platform” classification of Manzil’s service model has been confirmed.
- [ ] Data map: forms, AI prompts, analytics, maps, auth, CRM, and backup.
- [ ] Every processor, server jurisdiction, and transfer mechanism has been reviewed.
- [ ] Personal-data database registration / notification requirements have been reviewed.
- [ ] The cookie banner has been aligned with the actual technical cookies.
- [ ] Business profile and advertising labels have been developed.
- [ ] The review moderation, notice-and-action, and appeal processes are operational.
- [ ] The data-request channel for access, correction, withdrawal, and deletion is operational.
- [ ] The Uzbek/Russian/English pages are substantively synchronized.
- [ ] An Uzbekistan lawyer has reviewed the final texts.

## Important Notice

These documents are practical first drafts prepared for adaptation to the product. They are not legal advice or a completed compliance assessment. Before publication, have a lawyer licensed in Uzbekistan review the legal entity, operating model, data flows, advertising, and consumer terms.
