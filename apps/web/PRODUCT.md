# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, one storefront:

- **Business owners** (primary revenue) — Uzbek SME owners in Tashkent: restaurant, cafe, salon, service-shop operators. Context: deciding whether to claim/run their Manzil listing. They live in Telegram, not email; they're skeptical of "another directory." The site must earn a "claim my business" in one scroll.
- **Consumers** — people finding trusted places to eat, book, and visit. The web mostly points them to the mobile apps; discovery itself happens in-app.

## Product Purpose

Manzil is Uzbekistan's local business platform — the consumer app builds the audience, the business platform monetizes it (Free / Pro / Max). The marketing site is the top of the revenue funnel: it convinces an owner to register and a consumer to download. Success = a lead that registers a business, and a visitor who installs the app.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, rooted, quietly premium. Voice: plain, official, local — no hype, no marketing fluff, no emoji. Three words: **heritage · precise · confident.** It should feel like it was made in Tashkent, for Uzbekistan — not a generic global SaaS template translated into Uzbek.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS-template landings: cream/paper backgrounds, all-caps tracked eyebrows above every section, gradient-clip headline text, glassmorphism hero cards, identical icon-heading-text card grids, a "hero metric" big-number band. These are the AI tells to kill.
- 2GIS / Google Business Profile: utilitarian directory-with-a-dashboard. Manzil is warmer, more editorial, more local.
- Loud startup maximalism (Liquid-Death acid): wrong for a trust-first local platform.

## Design Principles

1. **Heritage as structure, not decoration.** The Samarkand arch/portal (the logo) is a load-bearing motif — section framing, the hero device — not a sticker.
2. **Say the real thing.** Official, common words; no invented marketing copy, no emoji, no "do this, do that" filler. One clear ask per fold.
3. **Committed color is voice.** Deep teal + gold carry the brand; don't hedge into beige-and-slate neutral safety.
4. **Motion enhances an already-visible page.** Every section renders fully without JS; motion is choreography on top, never a visibility gate.
5. **One moment per idea.** App download appears once, decisively — not repeated as bands and badges across every page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1 (verify teal/gold on surfaces). Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative for all motion. Trilingual (uz/ru/en) is core, not a feature — layouts must survive long Uzbek Latin words without overflow.
