# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Manzil Android is for consumers in Uzbekistan, starting with people in Tashkent who need to decide where to go, what to eat, where to meet, or which local service to trust. Users may be locals comparing familiar places, newcomers learning a district, families planning an outing, students/workers looking for a reliable cafe, or tourists who need local context quickly.

## Product Purpose

Manzil helps people discover trusted local businesses, compare options quickly, save places for later, write useful reviews, and ask an AI concierge for recommendations when they do not know exactly what to search. Success means users can answer "Should I go here?" from the mobile app without switching between maps, social media, and word of mouth.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, local, calm. The app should feel premium and practical: modern enough to be a daily platform, but rooted in Uzbek local life and business reality.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic directory apps with flat lists and no trust signals. Avoid over-decorated AI chat-first products where the assistant hides the core search workflow. Avoid tourist-brochure styling, heavy gradients, tiny labels, or layouts that break when Uzbek or Russian text wraps.

## Design Principles

- Trust before decoration: ratings, review count, verification, hours, distance, and useful summaries must be easier to find than visual flair.
- Search stays manual and fast: AI helps compress choices, but the app must still work as a clear directory.
- Local context is product value: districts, Uzbek language, family-friendly signals, halal cues, and practical details should shape every screen.
- Android-native behavior matters: bottom tabs, predictable back behavior, accessible touch targets, and adaptive layouts are part of the product quality.
- Every state teaches: empty, loading, permission, review, and saved states should tell users what to do next.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast where applicable, 48dp minimum tap targets, readable text at increased font sizes, reduced nonessential motion, and layouts that tolerate longer Uzbek Latin, Cyrillic, Russian, and English labels. Color must never be the only indicator for rating, verification, error, or saved state.
