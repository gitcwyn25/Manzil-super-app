The derivative photos are resized and compressed. The Chorsu Open Graph image
is also cropped to 1200×630. Chorsu derivatives remain available under CC BY-SA
4.0; retain attribution, license links and modification notices when reusing
them. The images do not imply endorsement by their photographers.

Manrope and JetBrains Mono retain the original design's typography and are now
served locally. Their unmodified font files and SIL Open Font License notices
are in `public/fonts/`; source URLs and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in
`docs/v3/evidence/font-provenance.json` at the repository root.

The four UI concept illustrations are owner-supplied synthetic product concepts,
not photographs of real application activity. Originals are preserved in
`source-assets/concepts/`; optimized versions are in `public/images/v3/`.
They carry visible illustrative-UI disclosures and do not substantiate ratings,
user counts, testimonials or verified business claims.
